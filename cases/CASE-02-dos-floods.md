This search measures the average number of packets transmitted per second for each attack. DoS Hulk has the highest packet rate by a large margin (~182,512 packets/s), demonstrating its objective of overwhelming the target server with excessive traffic. This evidence supports the classification of Hulk as an availability attack under MITRE ATT&CK T1498/T1499 (Network/Endpoint Denial of Service).
